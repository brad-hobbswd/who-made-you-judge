Continue the Journey

Helpful Resources for Continued Spiritual Growth

Finishing this book is not the end of the journey. If these chapters have encouraged you to think more deeply about truth, mercy, humility, and the character of Jesus Christ, then my prayer is that they also encourage you to continue seeking Him through His Word.

God's Word
is always
the greatest resource
for every believer.

No Christian book can replace the Bible. Every chapter of this book points back to Scripture because God's Word remains our authority for faith, doctrine, correction, and spiritual growth.

Recommended Bible Reading

If you would like to continue studying the themes found throughout this book, consider spending time reading and praying through these portions of Scripture.

Choose a Scripture card to read selected verses right here on this page. Each card also identifies the full passage so you can continue studying it in context.

The beginning of judgment

Genesis 3

Return to the story of the fall, the entrance of shame, and God's pursuit of humanity.

A heart made clean

Psalm 51

Explore repentance, mercy, and the prayer for a renewed heart.

What God requires

Micah 6:8

A clear call to do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with God.

Truth and humility

Matthew 5–7

Jesus teaches about the heart, mercy, reconciliation, and the danger of judging others.

Mercy toward others

Luke 6

Consider the standard of mercy Jesus calls His followers to live out.

Grace instead of condemnation

John 8

Reflect on the encounter with the woman accused by her neighbors and the words of Jesus.

The need for grace

Romans 2, 3, and 8

Read about impartial judgment, the universal need for salvation, and life without condemnation in Christ.

Saved by grace

Ephesians 2

Remember that salvation is God's gift, not something we earn or use to boast.

Faith that shows mercy

James 1 and 2

Study the connection between listening, humility, impartiality, and mercy.

Confession and cleansing

1 John 1

Be reminded of God's faithfulness to forgive and cleanse those who confess their sins.

Ways to Continue Growing

Healthy spiritual growth is not built upon information alone. It develops through a consistent relationship with Jesus Christ.

01

Stay in the Word

Make Scripture part of your daily life, not simply something you visit when a problem arises.

02

Build a prayer life

Bring your heart honestly before God and make room to listen as well as speak.

03

Worship and serve

Worship faithfully with your church family and serve others with humility.

04

Practice forgiveness

Choose to extend the mercy you have received and allow the Holy Spirit to shape your heart.

Questions for Reflection

As you continue your walk with Christ, return to these questions often.

Am I becoming more like Jesus?
Do I speak truth with love?
Have I laid down every stone?
Am I remembering the mercy God has shown me?
Do people encounter the grace of Christ when they encounter me?
Is humility shaping my relationships?
Am I allowing Scripture to transform my heart?

The journey does not end
with the final page.

It continues
every day
we choose
to follow Jesus.

Thank you for taking this journey with me. My prayer is that the Lord continues revealing Himself through His Word, strengthening your faith, and forming within you a heart that reflects His truth, His mercy, and His love.
